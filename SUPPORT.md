# Help with Recollect

[Email support: lemonthingllc@gmail.com](mailto:lemonthingllc@gmail.com?subject=Recollect%20Support)

Include your device model, operating-system version, Recollect version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot of the error can help; remove private journal content first. You do not need to send your journal to ask for help.

## Writing and photo access

Start in the Write screen to create an entry. If photo suggestions are missing, check Recollect's Photos permission in system settings. Limited Photos access means Recollect can only use the items you have allowed it to access.

## Intelligent features are unavailable

Recollect's intelligent prompts and conversations run on device. They require supported hardware and language, Apple Intelligence enabled in system settings, and the system model to be ready. The app reports when the device is not eligible, Apple Intelligence is disabled, or its model is not ready. Some writing features remain available without Apple Intelligence.

If the app reports an error or cancels repeatedly, send support the exact message and your device, language, and OS version. Your journal text is not needed to begin troubleshooting.

## An entry is missing or sync is delayed

Before deleting or reinstalling anything, check the devices on which the entry was created. Keep the device with the original content available. Check that the devices use the intended Apple account and that iCloud is available. Recollect's Settings includes iCloud Sync Status for more detail.

If an entry still appears missing, contact support with when it was created, which device saved it, and whether it appears on another device. Do not send private entry content unless you choose to.

## Export and backup

In Recollect, open **Settings → Import/Export**. **Create Shareable JSON Backup** prepares an export; **Export JSON Backup** lets you save or share it. **Back Up to iCloud Now** creates the app's JSON backup in iCloud when available.

This is a lightweight text-and-metadata export, not a complete archive of your photos, videos, and recordings. It is not a full-fidelity restore. **Import Backup File** is currently unavailable. Keep original media and your existing journal data; do not delete the app or a source journal based on the assumption that this JSON file can restore everything.

## Importing from Day One

Keep your original Day One journal and export until you have checked the imported entries and media in Recollect, including after closing and reopening the app. If an import fails, contact support with the error, approximate entry count, export format, and device details. Do not email the full journal export as the first troubleshooting step.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Use **Restore Purchases** on Recollect's purchase screen if you already purchased access, and check that your App Store account is the one used for the purchase. Contact support if access does not return.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple account. Deleting Recollect does not cancel a subscription. For an App Store refund request, use [Apple's Report a Problem website](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

## Privacy

Recollect does not send journal entries or prompts to third-party AI services for prompt processing. [Read how on-device intelligence, iCloud, purchases, and support work](../).
