# Remember the story behind the photo.

Your photos capture what happened. Recollect helps you remember what it meant.

Save a few words about an ordinary afternoon, a favorite person, or a trip you still think about. Add photos, videos, or a recording. A meaningful journal entry can be short.

[Get Recollect on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1617505834)

## Start with a moment

Photo suggestions and writing prompts give you somewhere to begin. Add the detail a photo cannot capture: what made you laugh, what someone said, or why you wanted to remember it.

## Keep your memories connected

Organize entries with journals, tags, and people. Find a memory by the person in it, browse your past entries, or return to the photos that bring a day back.

## Write it. Say it. Keep it.

Use text, photos, video, and audio to tell the story your way. Recording and transcription help when speaking feels easier than typing. Availability of intelligent features depends on your device, language, and system settings.

## Personal writing, on-device intelligence

Recollect's intelligent prompts and journal conversations run on device. Your journal entries and prompts are not sent to third-party AI services for prompt processing. iCloud sync and purchase management are separate services, explained in our [privacy policy](../).

## Try Recollect

Download the app and explore its journaling features. Some features require a subscription or lifetime purchase. The app shows the available options, local prices, and any eligible trial before you buy. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled.
